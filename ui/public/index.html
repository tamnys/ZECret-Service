The route of a request.

  1. You

How ZECret Service works

  1. Tor

    When you send a request, Tor keeps your IP address and other client details hidden from us and anyone else.

  2. TEEs

    Trusted Execution Environments run the RPC software, fetching your request while verifying that your data stays protected.

  3. Zcash blockchain

    Shielded pools on the Zcash blockchain return your encrypted data, so only you can decrypt your sensitive details.

    Use Rust 1.94.1 and the committed dependency lockfiles. You will also need Tor installed.

cargo build --locked --manifest-path tools/payment-crypto/Cargo.toml

    Get free tickets

    Request up to 100 tickets at a time through the client. No account or payment is needed. The client verifies them and keeps them on your device.

    Make a query

    The client checks the service through Tor before sending your request. Each live query uses one ticket. The verification files are included in the source download.

Privacy

What an RPC can learn about you.

Shielded Zcash transactions hide their sender, recipient, and amount on-chain. A direct RPC connection creates a separate privacy risk.

What a provider sees

The RPC provider can see your IP address and the methods and parameters you request, such as block or transaction IDs. Repeated lookups can reveal which activity interests you.

Why it matters

Query timing and patterns can be linked to an IP address and combined with other data. That can narrow anonymity, though a lookup by itself does not prove you own a transaction.

How ZECret Service addresses it

The native client is designed to hide your direct IP with Tor and verify the TEE and its live connection before sending a query. The TEE processes the request while limiting what the service operator can inspect. It does not make this website visit anonymous or eliminate every form of traffic analysis.
